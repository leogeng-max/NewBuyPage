Repeat Toggle — Concepts

Same idea as the checkbox on the buy flow — "this question is skippable right now, and you can also turn it off for next time" — explored as a range of interactions, not all of them dot-based. Tap any of them.

A · Dot only — the label reveals itself once you've tapped

Nothing to read until you've interacted with it once — after that, the label stays, and just reflects on/off.

B · Tap for a quick confirmation, then it recedes

Tapping confirms with the label for a moment, then it quiets back to just the dot. Worth flagging: this is the one option here that uses motion for feedback — everywhere else in this prototype deliberately avoids that.

C · Label always present, just quiet

No hidden state to discover — the label is always there, just small and low-contrast so it stays out of the way of the actual question above it.

D · Larger single ring, no separate checkbox glyph

Same "reveal on first tap" behavior as A, with a bigger, more deliberate ring instead of a small dot — reads as a single considered mark rather than a form control.

E · The title itself carries the state
Role

Tap the dot: it fills, the label reveals, and the title itself brightens to confirm this step is "on" for next time. Tap again and the title dims to gray, the dot empties — a quieter, in-place way of saying "this one's fading out of your future flow." The idea being explored: the on/off state doesn't need its own separate visual language, it can borrow the title's own weight.

F · A repeat/loop glyph instead of an abstract dot

Same reveal-on-tap behavior, but the mark is literally a repeat icon instead of an abstract dot — filled/lit when it'll come back, just an outline when it won't. More literal, less "which setting is this again?"

G · No separate control — the title is the tap target

No dot, no icon — tap the title itself. An underline appears under it while it's set to repeat; tap again and the underline goes away. The most minimal option here, at the cost of the tap target being less obviously interactive.

H · Square mark instead of a circle

Identical to A, swapping the circle for a small square — closer to the squared-off shapes used everywhere else in this prototype (chips, cards) instead of introducing a new, perfectly round form.

I · E + F combined — icon next to the title, confirms itself, then quiets down
Role

Starts lit (matches the real default — every field starts "on"). Tap it off and the icon fades to gray while a one-line "Hide next time" confirms what just happened, then the text recedes and the gray icon carries the state on its own. Tap it back on and "Repeats next time" flashes the same way. Unlike E, the title itself stays neutral — only the icon and its momentary label do the talking.
